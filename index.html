HOW IT WORKS

Your computer is the temporary bridge

  1. Create a room and scan its QR code, enter its code, or share the invite link.
  2. Choose text or a file on the device you’re sending from. The file picker opens on that device.
  3. Download on the other device. The server copy is removed three minutes after the download completes.
i

Temporary transfers, clear limits

Files pass through and are stored temporarily on the computer running SendGet. Files that are never downloaded are cleaned up after 30 minutes. A downloaded copy on a phone or computer is outside SendGet’s control and must be deleted on that device separately.
